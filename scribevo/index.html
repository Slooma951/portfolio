Scribevo

Writing help that understands how you spell.

Scribevo is a free Chrome extension for people with dyslexia. Type a word the way it sounds, like fone, becos or defnatly, and Scribevo works out what you meant. It fixes the whole sentence, and nothing changes until you accept it.

Add to Chrome, free Privacy policy

What it fixes

  • Phonetic spelling: fone, becos, skool, enuf
  • Missing and swapped letters: realy, freind
  • Missing apostrophes: dont, havnt, couldnt
  • Similar-sounding words: should of, your welcome, alot
  • Verb agreement and tense: he dont know, they was late
  • Capital letters and punctuation

Quiet by design

One suggestion at a time, with only the changed words marked. There's no score and no page full of red underlines. You can switch Scribevo off for any website, or pause it everywhere.

Help and feedback

Something not working, or have an idea? Email salem.elatrash@outlook.com. Scribevo started as a TU Dublin research project on dyslexia and writing, and feedback from the people who use it shapes what comes next.
